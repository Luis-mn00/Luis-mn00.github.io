Research on generative modeling in the Physics-Based Deep Learning group at TUM:

* **Sparse Flow Reconstruction:** Designed conditional guidance mechanisms for reverse diffusion to reconstruct full 2D/3D velocity fields from minimal discrete sensor probes, accurately resolving high-frequency turbulent structures (published in *Physics of Fluids*).
* **Physics-Constrained Flow Matching:** Applied continuous normalizing flows with exact conservation law guarantees to climate downscaling and super-resolution across turbulent atmospheric scales (published in *Machine Learning: Earth*).
