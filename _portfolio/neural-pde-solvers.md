Combining classical numerical methods with modern scientific machine learning:

* **Finite Element Solver (FEM):** Developed a finite element solver for Stokes equations governing creeping flows, establishing formal convergence proofs and a priori/a posteriori error estimates.
* **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs):** Investigated optimization landscapes, loss weighting strategies, and spectral bias in PINNs for solving ordinary and partial differential equations. Resulted in a comprehensive peer-reviewed tutorial in the *European Journal of Physics* and applications to photonics at CSIC.
