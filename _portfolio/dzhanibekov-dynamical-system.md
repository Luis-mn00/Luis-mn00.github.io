Exploration of the intermediate axis theorem (tennis racket effect / Dzhanibekov effect):

* Simulated Euler's equations of motion for an asymmetric rigid body experiencing torque-free rotation in 3D.
* Analyzed perturbation stability around principal axes of inertia, demonstrating the topological saddle-point instability of rotation along the intermediate axis.
* Generated interactive phase-space trajectories and visual demonstrations illustrating the periodic flip phenomenon.
