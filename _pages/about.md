I am a Doctoral Researcher at the **Technical University of Munich (TUM)** in the [Physics-Based Deep Learning (PBDL) Group](https://ge.in.tum.de/) led by Prof. Nils Thürey. My research focuses on **Scientific Machine Learning**, specifically developing foundation models and scalable deep learning architectures for **3D Neural PDE Solvers** and **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)** on unstructured meshes.

I hold a strong academic background in Physics, Mathematics, and Computational Science, with experience in high-performance computing (HPC) environments, distributed training, and multi-GPU cluster scaling for complex physical simulations.

---

### Research Interests

* **Foundation Models for Physical Systems**: Pre-training scalable neural surrogates that generalize across varied geometries and operational regimes.
* **Neural PDE Solvers on Unstructured Meshes**: Developing efficient Transformer architectures and operator learning techniques for high-resolution 3D domains (up to tens of millions of cells).
* **Geometric Deep Learning**: Exploiting geometric symmetries, intrinsic shape descriptors, and physics-driven representations.
* **Generative Models for Fluids & Climate**: Diffusion models and physics-constrained flow matching for super-resolution, sparse reconstruction, and climate downscaling.
