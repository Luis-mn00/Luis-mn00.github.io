Advanced seminar on cutting-edge deep learning methods applied to computer graphics and visual computing at TUM (Summer Semester 2026).

Topics discussed include neural radiance fields (NeRFs), 3D Gaussian Splatting, generative diffusion models for assets and motion, geometric deep learning on 3D meshes, and physics-based neural rendering.
