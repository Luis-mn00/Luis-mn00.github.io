Oral presentation on foundation models and neural operators for 3D partial differential equations (PDEs) on unstructured domains, delivered at the *International Symposium on AI and Fluid Mechanics* in Chania, Greece.

The talk covered strategies for data-efficient pre-training across diverse unstructured meshes, scaling transformer architectures to high-resolution physical domains, and future directions for physical surrogate modeling.
