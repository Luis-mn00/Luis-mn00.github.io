Oral presentation at the **World Congress on Computational Mechanics & ECCOMAS Congress (WCCM-ECCOMAS 2026)** in Munich, Germany.

Presented our novel framework for guiding conditional diffusion models to reconstruct 2D and 3D turbulent velocity fields from limited physical probe sensors, achieving superior fine-scale vortex resolution and energy spectrum fidelity compared to traditional estimation techniques.
